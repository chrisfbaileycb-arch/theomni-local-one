# OmniLocal #1 — Training Video Scripts (60 seconds each)

Record these on a phone, direct to camera. Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine), then paste the
links into the two video slots on the Strategy & Best Practices panel in the Content Director.

---

## Script 1 — "How to Run High-Converting Flash Campaigns" (~60s, ~150 words)

**[0:00–0:08] HOOK**
"Most local promotions fail for one reason: they never end. Here's how to run a flash campaign
that actually converts."

**[0:08–0:22] THE RULE**
"Rule one: every campaign gets a hard deadline — two to three days, max. 'This weekend only'
beats '10% off forever' every single time, because scarcity is what makes people move."

**[0:22–0:38] THE SETUP**
"Rule two: one offer, one channel, one window. Pick your slowest days, tease it the day before,
launch it, then shut it off. Inside OmniLocal, the Pacing Advisor tells you your best window,
and the calendar schedules the burst for you."

**[0:38–0:52] THE PROOF**
"Rule three: prove it. Every deal comes back through the scan-to-spin wheel with a POS code, so
your Weekly Win Report shows the real dollars — not likes."

**[0:52–1:00] CLOSE**
"Short. Sharp. Measured. Run bursts, not marathons — and watch the redemptions stack up."

## Script 2 — "Rules of Engagement for Gamification" (~60s, ~150 words)

**[0:00–0:08] HOOK**
"A prize wheel can flood your business with customers — or burn out your staff. The difference
is the rules of engagement."

**[0:08–0:24] RULE 1 — MYSTERY**
"Rule one: never show the prizes up front. It's a mystery wheel — everybody wins something, and
nobody knows what until they spin. That surprise is what makes whole households scan together."

**[0:24–0:38] RULE 2 — PRIVATE WINS**
"Rule two: no public winner announcements. Wins are revealed privately, so big prizes feel
exclusive and small prizes never embarrass anyone."

**[0:38–0:52] RULE 3 — LIMITS PROTECT YOU**
"Rule three: limits are your friend. One spin per person per week, coupons expire in seven days,
and repeat coupon-hunters get steered to the low-value deal automatically. Your margins stay safe."

**[0:52–1:00] CLOSE**
"Mystery, privacy, limits. Follow those three, and the game prints loyal customers — not losses."
